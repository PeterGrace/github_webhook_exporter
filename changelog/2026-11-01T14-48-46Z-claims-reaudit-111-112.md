# Claims re-audit of the #111 and #112 fixes

Independent re-audit of CLM-0195 (#111, fixed by #118) and CLM-0037 (#112, fixed by #119) at
`b763061`. The fix PRs had recorded their own `supported` verdicts. This pass gathered new
evidence with separate auditors (claude-sonnet-5-5), and claude-opus-5-5 reproduced the decisive
findings. Only `claims.json` changes.

## Results

- **CLM-0195: supported (confirmed).** `build_blocking_http_client` (`src/telemetry.rs:691-707`)
  errors on every redirect and is the only client behind the OTLP trace and log exporters.
  `production_client_rejects_redirects_without_contacting_the_target` passes: a 307 from the
  collector never reaches the redirect target. EVD-0299..0301, anchored as EVD-0306/0307.
- **CLM-0037: refuted again.** `WorkflowBranch::sanitize` rejects only `/`-delimited `.`/`..`
  segments, and `BRANCH_SEGMENT_ENCODE_SET` (`src/github/client.rs:43-53`) does not encode `\`.
  The `url` crate (2.5.8) treats `\` as `/` in http(s) URLs, so `a\..\..\orgs\x` becomes
  `/repos/o/r/orgs/x/protection/required_status_checks` and `..\..\orgs` becomes
  `/repos/o/orgs/...`. EVD-0303..0305; the claim now has a source anchor on the doc comment that
  promises otherwise (`src/github/client.rs:35-42`).

Parent roll-ups for CLM-0001, CLM-0004 and CLM-0005 were re-recorded; all remain refuted.
CLM-0058 (#113, fixed by #120) still carries only the builder's own verdict and was not re-audited
in this pass.
